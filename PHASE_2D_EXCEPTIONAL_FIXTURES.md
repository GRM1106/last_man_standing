# Phase 2D — Exceptional Fixture Outcomes

Phase 2D records selection-time provider status and observation time on each pick, adds an append-only administrator fixture-selection block ledger, and stores immutable pick-local automatic wins for fixtures that become postponed, abandoned or void after a valid lock. Raw football scores/status remain truthful; the LMS decision belongs to the historical pick and retains its original round and team cycle.

Provider-exceptional or administratively blocked fixtures reject new picks. A risk block alone never awards a win. When authoritative status later becomes exceptional, eligible unresolved picks snapshot the status/time as `auto_win`; processing treats that pick as won without inventing a score. A later rearranged fixture cannot rewrite the resolved historical pick.

Collective reinstatement, GW38, multiple winners, buy-back redesign, correction adjudication and scheduling remain out of scope.
